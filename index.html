Privacy Policy for Go Kids

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Go Kids is an educational app designed for children to learn through interactive activities and games.

Information We Collect

Go Kids does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses.

Children's Privacy

Go Kids is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Internet and Data

The current version of Go Kids does not require users to provide personal information to use its educational features.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Go Kids does not intentionally collect or share personal information with third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
